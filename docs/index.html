Key insights

  • Two slots cause 67% of all failed requests: airport pickups in the evening rush (only 20.7% fulfilled, 73% "no cars") and city pickups in the morning rush (28.1% fulfilled, 49% cancelled by drivers).
  • It is a direction problem: 3.94 city→airport requests per airport→city request in the morning, but only 0.30 in the evening. Cars pile up away from where the next demand is.
  • Cancellations are concentrated: the fifth of drivers who cancel most cause 31.5% of all cancellations (48% cancel rate vs 14% for the best fifth).
  • Opportunity: halving morning city cancellations and lifting airport evening fulfilment to 50% would add ~187 trips a day and raise fulfilment from 42.0% to ~55.9%.
